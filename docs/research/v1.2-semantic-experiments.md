# Architectures Evaluated for AnifLive-TTS v1.2

## Purpose

AnifLive-TTS v1.2 investigated whether reducing the serial work of the GPT
semantic autoregressive decoder could improve audible TTFA without reducing
speech quality. The production Transformer implementation remained the golden
baseline throughout the work. Experimental backends were eligible for release
only when they improved the measured end-to-end workload and passed the same
quality gates.

These findings apply to AnifLive-TTS, GPT-SoVITS V2ProPlus, the tested model
packages, and the implementation described here. They are not a general claim
that MTP or Mamba-2 is unsuitable for other TTS systems.

## Environment and protocol

- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Ti
- CUDA runtime: 12.8
- TensorRT: 11.2.1.2
- Precision: FP16
- Models: Miku V2ProPlus and Roxy V2ProPlus
- Request concurrency: 1
- Formal release measurement: 10 sessions per model; each model-session used
  10 warmups, 100 complete-WAV requests, 100 new-connection streaming
  requests, and 100 keep-alive streaming requests

The formal release result is recorded in
[`benchmarks/README_BENCHMARK_SUMMARY.json`](../../benchmarks/README_BENCHMARK_SUMMARY.json).
Architecture experiments used fixed seeds and separately preserved semantic,
latency, and quality reports. A theoretical reduction in FLOPs or NFE was not
accepted as evidence of an end-to-end improvement.

## Decision summary

| Candidate | Measured outcome | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Transformer, 1 token/NFE, TensorRT runtime optimization | Keep-alive audible TTFA P50 `77.717 ms`; all end-to-end and accepted quality gates passed | Adopted |
| Transformer + MTP-4 | Future-head accuracy and accepted-prefix length were insufficient; generated speech did not pass the semantic quality gate | Not adopted |
| 1:1 Transformer/Mamba-2 hybrid | Standalone plugin feasibility passed, but the trained AR student was slower and diverged from the teacher | Not adopted |
| Mamba-2 hybrid + MTP-4 | Its prerequisite hybrid and MTP candidates did not pass independently, so the combined path could not satisfy the joint gate | Not adopted |

## Selected production path

The selected v1.2 backend keeps the original 1-token/NFE Transformer decoder
and the established SoVITS acoustic path. Its measured improvement comes from
deployment and execution changes:

- fitted GPT engines for each model package;
- persistent TensorRT execution contexts and fixed KV buffers;
- zero TensorRT auxiliary streams for the fitted GPT path;
- GPU-resident sampling with a reusable CUDA Graph;
- the established 9+8 preview only for the first text segment;
- two-step EOS checks and a guarded 25-second warm window.

This path reduced keep-alive audible TTFA P50 from `89.296 ms` to
`77.717 ms` (`12.967%`) and complete-WAV RTF P50 from `0.128717` to
`0.088774` (`31.032%`). New-connection audible TTFA P50 improved from
`101.721 ms` to `96.034 ms`. All formal requests reported TensorRT 11 with no
PyTorch neural fallback.

## MTP-4

The MTP experiment froze the base GPT and trained future-token prediction
heads against captured semantic sequences. Its best validation top-1 accuracy
for the three future heads was:

| Future position | Top-1 accuracy |
|---|---:|
| t+2 | 44.61% |
| t+3 | 40.12% |
| t+4 | 38.45% |

GPU Viterbi selection improved local path choice but did not make four-token
commits reliable. On the held-out split, the selected configuration achieved
`30.07%` exact three-future-token paths and an accepted-prefix mean of only
`1.094` tokens. The resulting semantic output exhibited repetition and did
not satisfy the speech-quality regression gate. MTP-4 was therefore not
promoted into the production runtime.

This result does not rule out a differently trained conditional MTP design;
it only rejects the evaluated adapter and selection approach for v1.2.

## Mamba-2 hybrid

Mamba-2 was evaluated in two distinct stages.

First, an isolated CUDA 12.8 development container built a standalone
TensorRT 11 `IPluginV3` recurrent update. Engine build/deserialization,
`enqueueV3`, one-step and 64-step FP16 reference parity, and 4,096 recurrent
steps all passed on the RTX 5070 Ti. This proved that the deployment mechanism
was feasible; Windows and the production runtime image were not the blocker.

Second, a 1:1 Transformer/Mamba-2 autoregressive student was trained and
evaluated. Across 42 multilingual validation records:

| Metric | Transformer teacher | Hybrid student |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean execution time | 1.121 s | 3.566 s |
| Mean repetition rate | 0.1208 | 0.1482 |

Teacher/student semantic-token similarity was `0.1607`, and first-17-token
agreement was `0.2394`. The student was about `3.18x` slower in this research
evaluation and did not preserve the semantic sequence closely enough to enter
the production acoustic quality gate. The hybrid backend was rejected while
the standalone plugin feasibility result was retained.

## Combined Mamba-2 + MTP

The combined path depended on both a quality-preserving hybrid hidden space
and reliable multi-token future heads. Because neither prerequisite passed its
independent promotion gate, combining them could not recover the required
quality/performance balance. An end-to-end production backend was not enabled,
and no silent fallback was added.

## Why Transformer remained selected

Retaining Transformer was a measured decision rather than a lack of
architectural experimentation. The baseline-preserving TensorRT path produced
the best verified end-to-end result, preserved the known acoustic contract,
remained compatible with v1 model packages and API requests, and avoided an
unvalidated neural fallback. Future work can resume from the documented MTP
accuracy and hybrid-distillation limits without repeating the same experiments.
