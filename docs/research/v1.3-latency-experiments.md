# Architectures Evaluated for AnifLive-TTS v1.3

## Scope

The v1.3 latency program started from immutable v1.2 commit
`e8386758ac3629483c8ca761f42c3b96f58144e0`. Neural experiments ran through
Linux Docker on the RTX 5070 Ti with TensorRT 11. A candidate was rejected when
it failed the predeclared latency, continuation-reliability, or speech-quality
gate; theoretical operation counts were never treated as a release result.

The results below apply to the evaluated GPT-SoVITS V2ProPlus implementation,
training recipe, model packages, and workload. They do not establish a general
negative result for the underlying research technique.

## Evaluated Alternatives

### Hard-token acoustic lookahead

The candidate supplied authoritative semantic tokens plus uncommitted MTP
future tokens to the SoVITS preview path. A joint `N15+K2` policy passed the
bounded Japanese acoustic gate, but did not improve end-to-end latency:

| Model | v1.2 keep-alive TTFA | Candidate | v1.2 new connection | Candidate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Miku | 67.423 ms | 72.434 ms | 68.578 ms | 71.913 ms |

Future tokens were not committed to semantic history, but they still changed
the acoustic output seen by SoVITS. Cross-model quality and latency gates were
not met, so the path was not adopted.

### Recurrent speculative decoding

A conditional autoregressive drafter and TensorRT block verifier reduced target
model NFE. On the first-17-token critical path, however, wall time improved by
only `16.74%`, below the locked internal `25%` gate. Draft and verification work
consumed too much of the theoretical gain for this batch-1 workload.

### SemanticPiece / EABPE

Ninety frequency and merge configurations were evaluated. Full-sequence token
compression approached `45%`, but the compression did not occur where TTFA is
decided. Best first-17-token coverage was `29.41%` at Roxy P50 and `11.76%` at
Roxy P95; the worst language was `23.53%`, and Miku reached `23.53%`. The
candidate therefore did not materially reduce the serial first-preview path.

### FastStart distilled/pruned Transformer

The H17 TensorRT refill microbenchmark reached `2.605 ms` P50, but the
100-token continuation test produced eight strict failures. The segmented
`H4+H4+H4+H5` variant reached `10.019 ms` and produced 20 failures. Because
continuation correctness is a production requirement, neither variant was
eligible for release.

### D16 block diffusion

The evaluated D16 adaptation failed its semantic-quality gate. This result
rejects the tested low-rank adaptation recipe, not block-diffusion speech
generation as a class. The incomplete backend is not present in the v1.3
production runtime.

## Production Decision

v1.3 retains the v1.2 Transformer semantic decoder, TensorRT 11 FP16 execution,
9+8 first-preview policy, and SoVITS streaming path. It adopts the independently
qualified package-driven reference-expression system and local WebUI.

The canonical Roxy-only release benchmark measured keep-alive audible TTFA P50
at `74.578 ms` and complete-WAV RTF P50 at `0.087680`, compared with the v1.2
README values of `77.717 ms` and `0.088774`. Neutral complete WAV, streaming
PCM, and semantic outputs remain exactly equal to v1.2 across five languages
for both qualified packages.

No rejected experimental semantic backend, private model asset, checkpoint, or
training artifact is included in the production source or release bundle.
